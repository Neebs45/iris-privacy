Iris — Privacy Policy

Last updated: [date]

Iris is a personal media client application that connects to a Jellyfin media server that you provide and control. Iris is not operated as a public service.

Information stored on your device

To connect to your server, Iris stores the server address and the login credentials/authentication token you enter. This information is stored only on your device and is used solely to communicate with your server.

Information sent to others

Iris communicates only with the Jellyfin server address you configure. Iris does not send your personal information, credentials, or usage data to the developer or to any third party.

Analytics and tracking

Iris does not contain advertising, third-party analytics, or tracking.

Data retention and deletion

Information Iris stores on your device is removed when you uninstall the app. Your media library and account information reside on your Jellyfin server, which you control.

Children's privacy

Iris is a personal-use application and is not directed to children.

Changes

This policy may be updated; the "Last updated" date will reflect any changes.
